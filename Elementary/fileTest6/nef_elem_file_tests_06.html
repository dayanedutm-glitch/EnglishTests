Speaking

Part 1 · Your Home

Ask your partner about his / her flat or house, then answer your partner’s questions.

1. How many bedrooms are there?

2. Is there a garden?

3. Are there any plants in the living room?

4. Is there a garage?

5. Are there any pictures on the walls?

6. Where’s the bathroom?

7. Is there central heating?

Now ask your partner about the Empire State Building.
