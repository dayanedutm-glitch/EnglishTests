RULE: Subject + Adverb + Main Verb
POSITION RULES
Where does the adverb go?
• BEFORE main verbs: I always have breakfast.
(❌ NOT: Always I have breakfast / I have always breakfast)

• AFTER the verb 'to be': He is always late.
GOLDEN RULE
The "Never" Rule (No Double Negatives)
With never, ALWAYS use a positive [+] verb!

✅ He never eats meat.
❌ He doesn't never eat meat. (Forbidden in English!)
SECTION 4B QUESTION ARCHITECTURE
4B. Word Order in Questions
Master the two distinct question systems in English: questions with be (inversion) vs questions with other verbs (ASI & QuASI).
ASI: Auxiliary (Do/Does) + Subject + Infinitive
QuASI: Question Word + Aux (Do/Does) + Subject + Infinitive
QUESTIONS WITH BE

SECTION 4C MODAL AUXILIARY
4C. can / can't (+, —, ?)
Use can / can't for permission (allowed) and possibility (able/possible).
[+] S + can + V [—] S + can't + V [?] Can + S + V?
SPECIAL NOTE
The Three Uses of "You"
1. Singular (One person):
What do you do? $\rightarrow$ I'm a doctor.

2. Plural (Group of people):
Where do you live? $\rightarrow$ We live in Boston.

3. General / Impersonal (People in general):
You can't smoke here. $\rightarrow$ (= Nobody is allowed to smoke here).
