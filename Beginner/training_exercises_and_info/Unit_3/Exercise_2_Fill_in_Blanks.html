Auxiliary Agreement & Verb Forms

Type the correct auxiliary (do, does, don't, doesn't) or correct verb form in context.

1. Negation with 1st Person Singular (I)
Pronoun: INegation

I drink milk with my cereal. I prefer orange juice.

2. Wh- Question with 2nd Person (You)
Pronoun: youWh- Question

A: What time you start work every morning?
B: At half past eight.

3. Negation with 2nd Person (You)
Pronoun: youNegation

You eat meat because you are a vegetarian.

4. Yes/No Question with 3rd Person (Colin)
Pronoun: heQuestion

A: Colin work in an office?
B: Yes, he does.

5. Singular Noun Agreement: Our father
Subject: Our fatherPreposition + Noun (Singular)

Our father English very well at his job. (Hint: write "speak" or "speaks")

6. Plural Noun Agreement: My friends
Subject: My friendsPreposition + Noun (Plural)

My friends speak English; they speak Turkmen.

7. Negation with 3rd Person Singular (Gina)
Pronoun: sheNegation

Gina is an accountant, so she work in the kitchen.

8. Negation with 1st Person Plural (We)
Pronoun: weNegation

We speak Chinese, but we speak English and Russian.

9. Question with Plural Noun: The students
Subject: the studentsPreposition + Noun (Plural)

the students like the big sandwiches in the café?

10. Negation with 3rd Person Plural (They)
Pronoun: theyNegation

Bob and Diane have four coffee shops, but they sell meat or beer.
