📐 The 4 Master Formulas at a Glance:
(+) Affirmative
S + V / Vs + O
e.g., Their father works.
(-) Negative
S + don't/doesn't + V + O
e.g., Their father doesn't work.
(? y/n) Question
Do/Does + S + V + O?
e.g., Does their father work?
(w/h) Wh- Question
Wh + do/does + S + V + O?
e.g., Where does he work?
